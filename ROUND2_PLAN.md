# Round 2: search for useful examples, followed by independent confirmation

The first pilot remains unchanged. The user authorized continued development to find examples where conditioning outperforms projection. We will search for such examples explicitly; a selected example is evidence of existence, not an estimate of average superiority. Unsuccessful settings and search costs will be retained.

First execute the proposed shared-state contact-mode check on development seeds 400000–400015, at 3.2, 4.8 and 6.4 s. Draw 64 unmodified diffusion chunks per snapshot. Inspect all chunk trajectories under speed caps 150, 250 and 350 pixels/s. Continue the first 16 proposals, in proposal order without reward selection, to the normal 30 s episode limit. This bounds the initial check while leaving the 64-proposal bank available for additional independent conditional/projection comparisons. Report the uncertainty from that smaller continuation subset.

If the speed constraint remains weak, explore externally supplied workspace half-planes as virtual safety curtains, keeping the same native task reward, full 0.8 s horizon and globally convex projection. A fixed half-plane constrains the physical pusher center at every substep. Its physical interpretation is an inaccessible region of the workspace for the pusher. It does not certify the object's safety. Unlike a contact-rich nonlinear projection, this remains a global convex quadratic program because pusher position is affine in the actions. Both methods receive identical geometry and dynamics.

Development may select snapshots and constraint parameters for promising examples. Before testing their quality again, freeze each selected snapshot/geometry, the allowed snapshot perturbation distribution, observation history, sampler, initial chunk duration, continuation, score, effect threshold and confirmation seeds. Use fresh proposal and continuation randomness, plus fresh perturbed snapshots where feasible. Primary confirmation score is native mean reward over a fixed post-snapshot duration, with native maximum reward and strict completion also reported. A useful example requires an absolute mean-score gain of at least 0.05 with a paired 95% interval excluding zero, actual feasibility, and no advantage explained by hidden reference/solver failures. Record unsuccessful confirmation attempts as well.

Keep the released policy and its normalization/sampling unchanged. Use the first feasible proposal, never the highest-reward feasible one. Do not train a reward designed to prefer conditioning. If examples are found, distinguish these selected demonstrations from population-level or universal claims, and distinguish exact offline conditioning from a practical new diffusion sampler.
