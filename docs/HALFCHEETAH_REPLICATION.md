# Independent-model HalfCheetah replication, locked before new outcomes

This study follows the earlier setting-specific refinement advantage over BayesFP plus projection at RMS .72. The original finding was +30.61 native-return points, adjusted CI [5.44,57.42], for one trained model. This new study tests generalization across independently initialized/trained diffusion models; it is not another same-seed rerun.

Use eight new training seeds 120100–120107. Keep the already collected 128 complete episodes, group split, training-only normalization, architecture, 48,000-step final EMA recipe, early-state sampling and eight-action/.4 s chunk unchanged. Training randomness is independent across models; demonstration collection is not independently replicated. Do not select checkpoints or exclude models based on quality, safety or return. Report the base-policy first-chunk/common-continuation quality of every model, including weak ones.

Use the same 64 fresh simulator snapshots for all models: seeds 130000–130063, first 32 at reset and last 32 after exactly 1 s of the public SAC expert. Use four independent requests per state/model. Save full `mjSTATE_INTEGRATION` snapshots. Execute the complete .4 s chunk and then the same deterministic expert through 4 s total. Native reward/physics/termination are unchanged. HalfCheetah has no native unhealthy termination; progress and low-return outcomes must remain visible.

Freeze C={A in [-1,1]^48: RMS(A)<=.72}, first-feasible rejection with 128 independent proposals/request, exact global Euclidean full-chunk projection, the existing annealed gamma=.5 refinement (256/256/512 steps at DDPM indices 20/5/0), paper-derived BayesFP K=32/strength20, and BayesFP followed by the identical global projection. Include raw BayesFP violations. The safety-only cost, scheduler, score conversion and proposal-law postprocessing stay fixed. No fresh tuning. The constraint applies to the initial chunk's normalized actuator effort; it is not a whole-episode safety certificate.

The single primary contrast is **refinement minus BayesFP+projection**, equal weighting over eight models and 64 states. Report a 95% crossed bootstrap interval, resampling model indices and one shared state-index vector across models, with 20,000 draws. Also report the 95% Student t interval across eight model means (df=7), which conditions on the fixed state bank. Replication of a useful advantage requires both intervals above zero, mean >=26.188203 points (the original fixed useful-effect threshold), all eight models completed, zero hard-output violations and no rejection refusals. Show per-model results and positive/negative counts. Eight models give limited precision; do not infer universal superiority from this small replication.

Secondary descriptive contrasts are conditioning minus projection and refinement minus ordinary projection, with the same intervals and no independent confirmatory claims. All method outputs count. Report raw unsafe outputs, refusals, native failures, nonpositive-return outcomes, progress, acceptance, boundary concentration, pooled action MMD to rejection (RMS bandwidth .2), independent Q-versus-Q discrepancy, particle diagnostics, model training time and sampling cost. Refused conditional requests remain missing conditional outputs, not fabricated zero-action samples; any refusal prevents a successful full-study decision. No reward ranking or replacement seeds.

Before training, hash this protocol, config, scientific implementation, fixed demonstrations, expert archives and dependency record. Before evaluating each model, record its checkpoint hash. Consolidation must first pass numerical equivalence checks against the frozen sampler, refinement, BayesFP and simulator. Preserve every earlier experiment and the original positive result irrespective of this study's outcome. The new conclusion can be negative or inconclusive.
